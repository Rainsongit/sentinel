Paid time off: full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year, credited monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next year.

Requesting PTO: submit requests in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Your manager approves requests within 3 business days.

Sick leave is separate from PTO: employees get 10 sick days per year, and no advance notice is required.
